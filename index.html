Core Definitions & Shadow Mechanics

What is a Lunar Eclipse?

चन्द्र ग्रहण (Chandra Grahaṇa)

A lunar eclipse occurs when Earth moves directly between the Sun and the Moon, casting its shadow across the lunar surface. This alignment—called Syzygy—can only happen during a Full Moon (Pūrṇimā).

The Umbra (Core Shadow)

प्रच्छाया (Pracchāyā)

The Umbra is the central, darkest part of Earth's shadow where direct sunlight is completely blocked. When the Moon enters the umbra completely, a Total Lunar Eclipse occurs.

The Penumbra (Outer Shadow)

उपच्छाया (Upacchāyā)

The Penumbra is the lighter, outer region of Earth's shadow where sunlight is only partially obscured. A penumbral eclipse creates a subtle shading across the lunar disc.
